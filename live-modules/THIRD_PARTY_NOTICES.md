# Third-party notices

Some live modules adapt ideas, formulas and constants from open-source
projects. No files or assets were copied; these are the licences of the
projects the adapted parts come from.

## beatsaber.gd (Rhythm Sabers)

- **BeepSaber** by Holger Dammertz, https://github.com/NeoSpark314/BeepSaber
  (MIT). Adapted: placing each note from its beat time every frame, the cut
  plane through the blade's last two positions and the falloff of points
  with distance from the note's centre, the combo multiplier
  (1 + combo / 10, up to 8), swing size from recent tip travel, and pooled
  notes.
- **Moon Rider** by Supermedium, https://github.com/supermedium/moonrider
  (MIT). Adapted: testing the blade's swept path between two frames, the
  stroke direction over several frames, the direction limits (0.625 for a
  cut, 0.97 for full points), and the S A B C D F rank thresholds.
